When to replace

Four signs it is time.

If you spot any of these, it is worth having the tyres looked at.

  • Tread worn down to the wear bars. In Victoria the legal minimum tread depth is 1.5 mm.
  • Cracks, bulges or cuts in the sidewall
  • Uneven wear across the tread
  • A puncture that cannot be safely repaired
