Six things we do every day.

01

New tyres and fitting

We specialise in Pirelli and keep a range of other options, so there is a tyre to suit the car and the way you drive it. Fitting happens here in the workshop, on our own machines.

  • Worn tread
  • Upgrading
  • A full set or just a pair
Get a tyre quote
02

Wheel alignment

If the car pulls to one side, the steering wheel sits off-centre or the tyres are wearing unevenly, alignment is the first thing to check. We measure it and set it on our alignment hoist.

  • Pulling left or right
  • Off-centre steering wheel
  • Uneven tyre wear
Book an alignment
03

Wheel balancing

A shake through the steering wheel at speed often points to a wheel that is out of balance. We balance wheels on the machines in the fitting bay.

  • Steering wheel shake
  • Vibration at speed
Book balancing
04

Puncture repairs

Nail in the tread, or a tyre that keeps going soft? Bring it in. We will inspect the tyre and tell you plainly whether it can be safely repaired or needs replacing.

  • Slow leaks
  • Nails and screws
  • Low pressure warnings
Book a repair
05

Auto repairs

Beyond tyres, our mechanics look after auto repairs to industry standards. Call with the make, model and what you have noticed, and we will tell you how we can help.

  • Mechanical repairs
  • Something not feeling right
