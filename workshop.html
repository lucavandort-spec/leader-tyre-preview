About us

Leader Tyre & Automotive

We are a tyre and auto repair workshop at 163 Adderley St, West Melbourne. We specialise in Pirelli and keep a range of other options to choose from.

Our mechanics work with state-of-the-art equipment and to industry standards, and we go above and beyond on customer service. Trade and wholesale customers are welcome.
